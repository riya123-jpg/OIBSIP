Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India • 11th President of India

An aerospace scientist, visionary leader, and one of India's most respected personalities who inspired millions through his knowledge, humility, and dedication to the nation.

About Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He came from a modest family and grew up with strong values of discipline, hard work, and determination. Despite facing financial challenges during his childhood, he remained dedicated to his education and dreamed of serving the nation.

He studied Physics at St. Joseph's College and later completed Aerospace Engineering from the Madras Institute of Technology. His passion for science and technology laid the foundation for an extraordinary career in aerospace research and innovation.

Dr. Kalam worked with both DRDO and ISRO, where he played a key role in India's missile and space programs. He was one of the leading scientists behind the successful development of the SLV-3 launch vehicle and earned the title "Missile Man of India" for his remarkable contributions to defence technology.

In 2002, he became the 11th President of India and was widely known as the "People's President." Beyond science and politics, he inspired millions through his books, lectures, and vision for young minds. His life continues to motivate people to dream big, work hard, and contribute to society.

Journey Through Time

  1. 1931

    Born in Rameswaram

    Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, into a modest family. His humble beginnings shaped his values of hard work, discipline, and perseverance.

  2. 1954

    Graduated in Physics

    Completed his Bachelor's degree in Physics from St. Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli, laying the foundation for his scientific journey.

  3. 1960

    Completed Aerospace Engineering

    Graduated from the Madras Institute of Technology (MIT), where he developed a deep interest in aerospace engineering.

  4. 1969

    Joined ISRO

    Joined the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) and contributed to the development of India's first indigenous Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-3).

  5. 1980

    SLV-3 Success

    Successfully led the launch of the SLV-3, placing the Rohini satellite into orbit—a major milestone in India's space program.

  6. 1998

    Pokhran-II Nuclear Tests

    Played a key role in India's successful Pokhran-II nuclear tests, strengthening the country's defence capabilities.

  7. 2002

    Became the 11th President of India

    Served as the 11th President of India and earned the title "People's President" for his humility and connection with students and citizens.

  8. 2015

    A Legacy That Lives On

    Passed away on 27 July 2015 while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong, continuing to inspire millions through his vision, books, and lifelong dedication to education and nation-building.
